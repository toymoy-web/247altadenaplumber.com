A slow or backed-up drain is one of the most common calls we get in Altadena, and it ranges from a single clogged sink to a main line backing up into more than one fixture at once. The right fix depends on where the clog sits and what caused it.

What Causes Drain Clogs In Altadena Homes

Many Altadena properties still run on original clay or cast iron sewer laterals from the early-to-mid 20th century. Both materials are vulnerable to root intrusion, and Altadena's mature deodar cedars and oaks (the same trees that line the historic Christmas Tree Lane on Santa Rosa Avenue) have root systems that can find their way into an aging clay joint over enough years.

Grease buildup in kitchen lines and mineral scale from the area's hard water add to the problem inside the house, narrowing pipe diameter gradually until a clog forms at the point of least resistance, usually a joint, elbow, or trap.

Signs Of A Drain Or Sewer Line Problem

Slow Drainage

Water taking longer than usual to clear a sink, tub, or shower is often the first sign of a building clog.

Multiple Fixtures Backing Up

If more than one drain is slow at the same time, the problem is likely in the main line, not an individual fixture.

Gurgling Sounds

Air trapped behind a partial clog can cause a gurgling noise in nearby drains or toilets.

Sewage Odor

A persistent sewer smell indoors or in the yard can point to a cracked or blocked lateral line.

How We Diagnose And Clear It

A drain snake or auger clears most single-fixture clogs quickly. For a recurring clog or a main line backup, we run a sewer camera through the line first to see exactly where the blockage sits and what caused it, rather than guessing.

Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the full diameter of the pipe, which clears grease and scale buildup that a snake can only punch a hole through. It is the more thorough option for a line that keeps clogging in the same spot.

Any repair beyond clearing the clog, such as a section replacement, follows Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) standards for drain and sewer pipe sizing and slope.

Clearing The Line vs. Fixing The Cause

Snake Or Auger

Fast and effective for an isolated clog with no underlying pipe damage.

Hydro Jetting

Better for grease buildup or a line that clogs again within a few months of snaking.

Camera-Confirmed Repair

If the camera shows a cracked pipe or heavy root intrusion, clearing the clog is a short-term fix until the pipe itself is addressed.

Common Questions

Why does my drain clog again a few weeks after snaking?

A snake clears a path through a clog but does not remove grease or scale coating the rest of the pipe. Hydro jetting clears the full diameter and holds longer.

Is a slow drain an emergency?

A single slow drain usually is not, but a backup affecting multiple fixtures or a sewage smell indoors should be treated as urgent.

Do tree roots really get into sewer lines?

Yes, especially into older clay or cast iron laterals with aging joints, which is common in Altadena's pre-1970s housing stock.

Before We Arrive

  • Stop running water into the affected drain, including the dishwasher or washing machine if a kitchen or laundry line is backing up. Adding more water can push a partial clog into a full backup.
  • If more than one fixture is affected, avoid using any drain in the house until the technician arrives. That keeps the situation from spreading while the main line is diagnosed.
  • Note which fixtures are slow and which are draining normally. That pattern helps us narrow down whether the clog is close to one fixture or further down the main line.
