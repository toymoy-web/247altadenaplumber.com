The water service line runs from the meter or curb stop to your home, separate from the interior plumbing repiping covers. A leak or failure on this line affects your entire home's water supply at once.

Why Service Lines Fail In Altadena

Altadena's mix of clay, sandy loam, and adobe soils shifts with seasonal moisture, and a rigid service line running through that movement over decades can develop stress cracks or joint separation.

Because Altadena is served by a mix of mutual water companies rather than one utility, service line materials and age can vary block to block depending on when that particular company's infrastructure was installed or last upgraded.

A property near the boundary between two water companies' service areas can also have a longer or more complex service line run than a neighbor closer to that company's main distribution point, which affects both diagnosis and repair cost.

Signs Of A Service Line Problem

Low Pressure Throughout The House

Unlike a single fixture issue, a service line problem affects every fixture in the home at once.

Wet Area Near The Meter Or Curb

Standing water or unusually green grass along the line's path can indicate a leak.

Unexplained High Water Bill

A leaking service line runs continuously and can account for a significant unexplained increase in usage.

Discolored Water After Utility Work

Nearby utility or street work can sometimes disturb an aging service line, causing sediment or discoloration afterward.

How We Diagnose A Service Line Issue

We start at the meter to confirm whether the leak is on the utility side or the property owner's side of the connection, since responsibility and repair scope differ between the two.

Any replacement section uses materials meeting Uniform Plumbing Code standards for buried water service lines, sized correctly for the property's fixture count and distance from the meter.

Acoustic detection traces the line's path underground to narrow the leak's location before any excavation, the same method used for a household slab leak.

Repair Vs. Replacement

Spot Repair

A single, well-located leak on an otherwise sound line can often be repaired without replacing the full run.

Full Line Replacement

An aging line with multiple issues, or one made of an outdated material, is often better replaced entirely.

Trenchless Option

Depending on the line's path and length, a trenchless replacement can avoid a full trench from the meter to the house.

What Affects The Cost Of Water Line Repair & Replacement

Scope Of The Problem

A straightforward, isolated repair costs less than a job involving multiple fixtures or a harder-to-diagnose root cause.

Parts & Materials

Fixture quality, pipe material, and whether a part needs to be special-ordered all factor into the final price.

Access & Location

A repair behind a finished wall, under a slab, or in a hard-to-reach crawlspace takes longer than one with open access.

Timing

Standard daytime scheduling costs less than an after-hours or weekend emergency dispatch.

Homeowner's insurance often covers sudden, accidental damage like a burst pipe, but typically does not cover gradual wear, an aging part failing on its own, or deferred maintenance. Check your specific policy for what it actually covers before assuming either way.

Common Questions

Who is responsible for the water line, me or the water company?

This depends on which mutual water company serves your property and where the meter sits. We confirm this at the start of any service call so you know your responsibility.

How is a service line leak different from a slab leak?

A service line leak is between the meter and the house, before water enters the home's interior plumbing. A slab leak is inside the home's own supply lines running under the foundation.

Can a service line be replaced without digging up my whole yard?

In many cases yes, using a trenchless method, depending on the line's length and path.

How quickly does a service line leak need attention?

It depends on severity, but since it affects your entire home's water supply and can run continuously, a suspected service line leak is worth scheduling promptly rather than waiting.

Before We Arrive

  • Locate your water meter if you can, since that is the starting point for diagnosing whether a leak is on the utility side or your property's side.
  • Note whether low pressure affects every fixture in the house at once or just a few, since that distinction points toward or away from a service line problem.
  • If you have recent water bills showing a jump in usage, have those available for reference.
