An already-installed tankless water heater needs different ongoing service than a tank unit. Most performance problems trace back to mineral scale buildup inside the heat exchanger rather than a failing component.

Why Tankless Units Need Regular Service In Altadena

Tankless units heat water on demand by passing it through a heat exchanger, and Altadena's hard water leaves mineral scale on that exchanger's internal surfaces faster than it would in a softer-water area. Manufacturers typically recommend annual descaling specifically because of this.

A unit that has never been descaled since installation is one of the most common reasons we see reduced flow, inconsistent temperature, or an error code on a tankless system that is otherwise mechanically sound.

Which mutual water company serves a given property can make a noticeable difference in how quickly scale accumulates, so a unit on one side of Altadena may need service more often than an identical unit a few blocks away on a different provider's line.

Signs Your Tankless Unit Needs Service

Error Code Displayed

Most tankless units display a specific error code that points to the affected component, which we can look up and diagnose directly.

Inconsistent Water Temperature

Fluctuating temperature during use often points to scale restricting flow through the heat exchanger.

Reduced Flow Rate

A drop in how much hot water the unit can deliver at once is a common sign of internal scale buildup.

Unit Never Descaled

A tankless unit installed more than a year ago with no descaling service is due, regardless of current symptoms.

How We Service A Tankless Unit

Descaling flushes a vinegar-based or manufacturer-approved solution through the heat exchanger using the unit's built-in service valves, which clears mineral buildup without needing to disconnect the unit.

For an error code, we reference the specific manufacturer's code definitions rather than guessing, since the same code can mean different things across different tankless brands.

Venting and combustion safety on gas tankless units follow the same ANSI Z21.10.3 safety standard every certified gas water heater is tested against, which is part of why venting checks are included whenever an error code points to an airflow issue.

Common Questions

How often should a tankless water heater be descaled?

Most manufacturers recommend annual descaling in hard water areas like Altadena, more often if the unit shows early scale-related symptoms.

Can I descale a tankless unit myself?

Some homeowners do with a kit, but a professional service confirms the solution and process match your specific unit and checks for other issues at the same time.

What does it mean if my tankless unit shows an error code?

It depends on the brand and specific code. We look up the manufacturer's definition and diagnose the actual component before recommending a repair.

Is a tankless unit more prone to scale than a tank water heater?

The heat exchanger's smaller internal passages make it more sensitive to scale restriction than a large tank, which is why manufacturers set a more frequent descaling schedule for tankless units in hard water areas.

Can a neglected tankless unit be restored to full performance?

In most cases yes, provided the scale has not caused permanent heat exchanger damage. A descaling service alone resolves the majority of performance complaints we see.
