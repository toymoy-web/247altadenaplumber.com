Water Service In South Pasadena

South Pasadena runs its own municipal Water Division, in service since the city's 1888 incorporation, drawing from groundwater wells in the Main San Gabriel Basin along with imported water from the Metropolitan Water District. That is a different setup than Altadena's patchwork of smaller mutual water companies, though both areas deal with a mix of groundwater and imported supply.

South Pasadena Neighborhoods We Serve

South Pasadena is best known for its well-preserved Craftsman and Mission Revival homes, many dating to the early 1900s, and for Mission Street's historic downtown business district. The city's decades-long fight against the 710 Freeway extension, which the Metropolitan Transportation Authority formally withdrew support for in 2017, is part of why so much of that original housing stock and street grid survived intact.
