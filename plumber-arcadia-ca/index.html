Water Service In Arcadia

Arcadia is served primarily by its own city-owned water utility, Arcadia Water Services, in operation since 1903 and drawing from groundwater wells in the Main San Gabriel and Raymond basins, with Golden State Water Company serving a portion of the city's southern section. That two-provider setup is different from both Altadena's mutual companies and Sierra Madre's single municipal department next door.
