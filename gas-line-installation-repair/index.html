Gas line work is not a do-it-yourself job. Whether it is a suspected leak, a line for a new appliance, or rebuild plumbing after the Eaton Fire, gas piping has to be installed and pressure-tested to code by a licensed plumber.

When Altadena Homes Need Gas Line Work

Rebuild projects after the Eaton Fire commonly need entirely new gas piping run to the meter, sized correctly for every appliance the rebuilt home will have, including a tankless water heater if one is planned.

Older homes sometimes have gas lines sized for fewer appliances than they now run. Adding a gas range, a tankless water heater, or a fire pit to an existing system without checking total BTU demand against line capacity is a common cause of pressure problems.

A gas line disturbed during nearby trenching or landscaping work, even work unrelated to plumbing, is also worth having inspected, since a nicked or stressed line does not always leak immediately at the point of damage.

Signs Of A Gas Line Problem

Smell Of Gas

A sulfur or rotten-egg odor near an appliance or the meter needs immediate attention. Leave the area and call from outside.

Hissing Sound Near A Line

An audible hiss near gas piping or a fitting can indicate an active leak.

Appliance Not Reaching Full Output

A gas range or water heater not reaching expected heat output can mean the line is undersized for total household demand.

Dead Or Discolored Vegetation

Unexplained dead patches near an underground gas line can be a sign of an underground leak.

How We Handle Gas Line Work

Any suspected leak is checked with a gas leak detector and soap-solution testing at fittings and joints, never by smell alone, before any repair work begins.

New installations and repairs are pressure-tested per California Plumbing Code gas piping provisions before the line is put into service, and every job goes through LA County permit and inspection.

Common Questions

What should I do if I smell gas in my house?

Leave the house immediately without operating any switches or appliances, and call from outside or from a neighbor's property. Do not attempt to locate the leak yourself.

Do I need a permit for new gas line work?

Yes. Gas line installation and most repairs require an LA County permit and inspection, which we handle as part of the job.

Can my existing gas line handle a new tankless water heater?

It depends on total BTU demand across all appliances. We calculate this before installation to confirm whether the existing line has capacity or needs upsizing.

How long does a gas line inspection take?

A standard leak check and pressure test is usually completed within an hour, though a full new installation or repair takes longer depending on scope.

Can I smell a small gas leak, or does it need equipment to detect?

Small leaks are not always detectable by smell alone, especially outdoors or underground. That is why we use a gas leak detector and soap-solution testing rather than relying on odor.

What To Do If You Smell Gas

  • Leave the building immediately. Do not operate light switches, appliances, or anything electrical on your way out.
  • Do not attempt to locate or fix the leak yourself, and do not light a flame anywhere near the suspected area.
  • Call from outside the home, or from a neighbor's property, once you are safely away from the building.
